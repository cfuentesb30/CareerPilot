## 🎨 **How to Add Your Logo**

### **Step 1: Add Logo File**
Place your logo file in `/app/frontend/public/` directory:
```
/app/frontend/public/
├── logo.png (or logo.svg)
└── favicon.ico
```

### **Step 2: Update Logo Component**
In `/app/frontend/src/App.js`, replace the placeholder:

**Current Placeholder:**
```jsx
<div className="w-12 h-12 bg-white/20 rounded-xl flex items-center justify-center backdrop-blur-sm border border-white/30">
  <span className="text-2xl font-bold text-white">CP</span>
</div>
```

**Replace with Your Logo:**
```jsx
<div className="w-12 h-12 rounded-xl overflow-hidden">
  <img 
    src="/logo.png" 
    alt="CareerPilot Logo" 
    className="w-full h-full object-contain"
  />
</div>
```

### **Step 3: Update Favicon (Optional)**
Replace `/app/frontend/public/favicon.ico` with your brand favicon.
